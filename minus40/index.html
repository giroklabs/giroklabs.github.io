Minus 40 Privacy Policy

Effective: November 18, 2026

Information we collect

Minus 40 ("the app") does not collect or transmit any personal information, including names, email addresses, device identifiers, location or usage data. There is no account or sign-in, and the app uses no advertising or analytics tools.

Data stored on your device

Game progress such as your saved run, best records, settings (sound, haptics, units) and discovered easter eggs is stored only on your device and is never sent to anyone, including the developer. It is removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

The full game unlock is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. The app never receives your payment details; it only checks with the store, on your device, whether the purchase was made. Payment information is governed by each store's privacy policy.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
